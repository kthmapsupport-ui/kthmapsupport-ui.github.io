KTH Map open map data

Contains information from OpenStreetMap, which is made available here under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. © OpenStreetMap contributors.

The street map, the walking network, the entrances, the places and the addresses of KTH Map are drawn and computed on the phone from data files that are derived from OpenStreetMap. This page offers those files to everyone, under the same licence, as the licence asks.

Download

  • kth-map-osm-data.zip (the data files and a README with the format of each file)
  • README.txt (the same text as inside the zip)

What you may do

Copy, distribute and adapt these files under the ODbL: credit OpenStreetMap, say so if you changed them, and offer what you publish from them under the same licence. Some buildings were added from Overture Maps, which is also under the ODbL; its contributors include Esri Community Maps contributors (CC BY 4.0), Microsoft (ODbL) and Google (CC BY 4.0).

What is not included

The rooms, the floor plans and the names taken from KTH Places belong to KTH and are not part of this offer. In the files of gyms and scooter parking the field sources names where each kind of entry comes from: only the OpenStreetMap part is offered under the ODbL, the other sources keep their own terms.
