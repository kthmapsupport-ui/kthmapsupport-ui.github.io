Campus Map open map data

The street map, the walking network, the bicycle network, the entrances, the places and the addresses of Campus Map are drawn and computed on the phone from data files that are derived from OpenStreetMap. This page offers those files to everyone, under the same licence, as the licence asks.

The bicycle network

bike_graph.bin is the network the app uses to work out routes by bike, e-bike and e-scooter: the cycle ways, paths, footways, service roads and streets of greater Stockholm and of the surroundings of Södertälje and Haninge, as nodes and edges. Every edge says what kind of way it is (cycle way, shared path, street with a lane, quiet street, street, busy road, main road, or a stretch where the rider walks with the machine), its surface, whether it is one way for bicycles, and whether it is lit; some nodes are traffic lights, crossings or barriers. It is a binary file made from OpenStreetMap by build_bike_graph.py with the rules of bike_classify.py; the README in the zip has the format (bike_graph_codec.py, which also holds a reader and a writer) and the rules.

The places to park a bike

bike_parking.json lists the places where a bike, an e-bike or an e-scooter can be left near the campuses (bicycle parking and the charging stations for bikes of OpenStreetMap): the position, whether there is a roof, lockers or a charger, how many bikes fit, whether it is paid and whether it is for everybody. It was made by build_bike_parking.py, whose first lines describe the format; the places marked private in OpenStreetMap are only listed by their identifier, so that the app does not send anybody there.

What is not included

The rooms, the floor plans and the names taken from KTH Places belong to KTH and are not part of this offer. In the files of gyms and scooter parking the field sources names where each kind of entry comes from: only the OpenStreetMap part is offered under the ODbL, the other sources keep their own terms.
