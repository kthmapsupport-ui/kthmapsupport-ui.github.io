Campus Map privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026. Campus Map is an independent app for university students. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by KTH Royal Institute of Technology or SL.

1. Who is responsible

The developer of Campus Map (the name and country of the developer are shown in the store listing). Contact: kthmap.support@gmail.com.

2. Short version

The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no server of ours. Your timetable, settings, home point and activities stay on your phone. We do not receive or store any of them. To do its job the app sends some requests straight from your phone to the services listed below. The street map makes no requests at all; the optional satellite view (off until you switch it on) is made by Google's Maps SDK and does contact Google, see below.

Satellite view (Google Maps). Only while you use the satellite view, Google's Maps SDK, inside the app, asks Google for the photo of the area you are looking at and Google receives, according to its documentation of the SDK: your phone's IP address, the model and system version of the phone and the version of the SDK, crash reports of the SDK, a pseudonymous identifier of the SDK (to count daily users), and which part of the map you move and zoom to. If you allowed location, the blue dot that shows where you are on that map is drawn by the SDK on your phone. Google acts as an independent controller of this data under its own privacy policy; we receive none of it. The app does not give Google your timetable, your home point or your activities. If you do not switch the satellite view on, nothing is sent to Google. The legal notices of the SDK are in the app (Settings, About).

Every web request exposes your phone's IP address to the site that receives it, as with any app. Those sites have their own privacy policies. The app does not request background location.

4. Legal basis and retention

Processing happens on your device or at your request (contract and your consent when you allow location). We keep nothing on our side. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone.

5. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can delete everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. You may contact us at the address above, and you may complain to your data protection authority (in Sweden: IMY, imy.se).

6. Children

The app is meant for university students and is not directed at children.

7. Changes

If the app starts to process data differently, this page will be updated and the date above changed.
